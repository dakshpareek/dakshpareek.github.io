Current scope

A fixed cartoon cast and action library, JSON authoring, and a local command-line renderer. A hosted API, customer editor, arbitrary characters, and general-purpose film generation are not demonstrated.

Potential pilot: test one recurring short-video format with three scenes and one targeted revision per scene. Agree on visual fit, rights, and acceptance criteria before implementation.
